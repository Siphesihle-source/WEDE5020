About Simza's Car Wash

Learn more about our business, mission and vision.

Our History

Simza's Car Wash is a small growing car wash business in Johannesburg. Steven Nkosi started the business in 2022 with the aim of providing affordable prices and quality car cleaning services to local customers.

Our Mission

Simza's Car Wash aims to provide reliable and quality vehicle cleaning services while ensuring the best customer service.

Our Vision

The vision of Simza's Car Wash is to become one of the most trusted and recognised car wash businesses in Johannesburg by offering various services.

Our Customers

Our website and services are designed to support different types of customers, including:

  • Employed people who need quick and reliable car wash services.
  • Families with one or more cars.
  • Taxi and e-hailing drivers who require consistent car washing.
